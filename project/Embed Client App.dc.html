Assumptions
  • Customers are invited by their Embed lead; no self-signup. Magic link, no passwords.
  • New projects start from a 4-step brief (name, services, goal, budget range, timing) → discovery call → fixed-price proposal within 3 working days. Until then the project shows a pre-Phase-1 state on Home.
  • Scope quotes are fixed price, human-reviewed, returned in-app within one working day; 50/50 billing.
  • FX shown at an indicative rate (₹83.3 ≈ $1); invoices settle in the contract currency.
  • Deep content (deliverables, approvals, quotes) is modelled on one project; the switcher changes the header, phase and progress.
  • Preview thumbnails and Figma links are placeholders pending real artefacts.
